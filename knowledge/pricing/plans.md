# Plans

Flowbase has three public plans:

- Starter: USD 29 per month, includes 3 seats, 5 active automations, and 10,000 monthly runs.
- Growth: USD 99 per month, includes 10 seats, 50 active automations, and 100,000 monthly runs.
- Enterprise: Custom annual contract, includes SSO, dedicated success support, audit exports, and negotiated run volume.

Extra Growth seats cost USD 12 per seat per month. Starter cannot buy extra
seats; customers needing more than 3 seats should upgrade to Growth.

Refund eligibility is governed by the [refund window](../policies/refund-window.md).
