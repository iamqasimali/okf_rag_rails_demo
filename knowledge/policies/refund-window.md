# Refund Window

Flowbase offers refunds for self-serve subscriptions within 14 calendar days of
the original purchase or renewal date. The workspace must have processed fewer
than 500 automation runs during that billing period, and the account owner must
submit the request from the billing email address.

Annual contracts signed through sales are not automatically refundable. They can
be reviewed only when the customer reports a duplicate charge, failed provision,
or documented billing error.

Plan downgrades take effect at the next renewal. Agents should link customers to
[Flowbase plans](../pricing/plans.md) before recommending a downgrade.
